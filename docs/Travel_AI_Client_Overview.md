# Unipro Travel AI Client Brief

## Purpose

Unipro Travel AI gives corporate travel teams one guided workspace for request intake, itinerary planning, disruption recovery, approval review, and final handoff.

The product is designed for operations teams that receive travel details from many places: forms, PDFs, spreadsheets, emails, policy documents, and follow-up messages. Instead of forcing the team to stitch those details together manually, Travel AI turns the request lifecycle into a visible, reviewable workflow.

## The Client Problem

Corporate travel work is rarely blocked by a single missing tool. It is usually slowed by scattered information, repeated context checks, unclear request status, and rushed follow-up when travel plans change.

Common issues include:

- Travel requests arriving in different formats.
- Company policy and traveler context being checked manually.
- Flight and hotel options being compared without a consistent review path.
- Managers lacking a clear view of which requests are pending, ready, or blocked.
- Disruptions requiring ad hoc recovery notes and repeated communication.
- Final itinerary handoff being assembled manually.

## The Solution

Travel AI creates an operating layer for business travel. Managers upload the company context, requests enter a structured queue, agents work through a guided itinerary builder, and reviewed itineraries are prepared for downstream booking and traveler communication.

The value is not in replacing existing booking providers or approval rules. The value is in making the work easier to manage, easier to review, and easier to hand off.

## What the Workflow Covers

1. Manager uploads company context.
   The manager provides the employee workbook and company policy PDF so the workspace has the right operating context.

2. Requests enter the queue.
   Uploaded forms or manually created requests appear in a structured travel operations queue.

3. Agents build the itinerary.
   The guided workspace helps the team review missing details, compare flight options, select hotels, and prepare the itinerary.

4. Recovery stays inside the same workflow.
   When a flight is cancelled or a critical issue appears, the team works from a recovery-focused path instead of starting over.

5. Approval and export complete the handoff.
   The reviewed itinerary package is ready for downstream booking, traveler communication, and operational follow-up.

## Client Outcomes

- Faster handling of travel requests.
- Clearer manager visibility across pending and ready requests.
- More consistent use of company context during planning.
- Cleaner recovery handling when plans change.
- Better handoff from manager upload to agent execution.
- A more professional itinerary package for downstream use.

## Positioning

Travel AI is a corporate travel operations workspace. It supports the team from intake through review and handoff while keeping existing booking and approval processes intact.
